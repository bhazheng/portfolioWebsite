## Design Specificity Verdict

**Status: Severe Drift from Stated Identity.**
DESIGN.md explicitly committed to "The Trader's Ledger" and declared the previous dark-glass "translucent modern tech" world as anti-reference. Yet the current codebase is still overwhelmingly executed in dark frosted glass (`bg-glass`, `shadow-glass`, `backdrop-filter: blur(18px) saturate(150%)`). The rubber stamp system, red correction strokes, and ruled ledger tables promised in the design specification have not replaced the glass cards and generic pill badges.

Most critically, the differentiator for Akbar Lucky Basuki is **Data Storytelling**. The hero visualization (`HeroDashboard.vue`) is supposed to be the definitive proof of that skill. Instead, it currently has empty strings for `title`, `caption`, `narrative`, `yAxisLabel`, and `legend`, cycling unlabelled SVG paths every 4 seconds. An analyst whose hero chart has no labels or context conveys the exact opposite of rigorous data storytelling.

3. **Structured Capabilities in SkillsView**: Grouping skills by category with explicit `appliedIn` project context transforms a standard list into genuine analytical evidence.

## Priority Issues

### [P0] Critical Contrast Bug: Invisible Highlight Text in Projects
- **What**: In `ProjectsView.vue` (lines 24 and 39), key highlighted metrics use `text-ink` (`#100b08`) on dark card surfaces (`rgba(39, 29, 21, 0.45)`).
- **Why it matters**: The most important proof points ("RCA" framework and "91%" accuracy) are rendered as black text on near-black background, completely invisible to recruiters and visitors.
- **Fix**: Replace `text-ink` with `text-paper` or `text-brass`.
- **Suggested command**: `$impeccable colorize components/ProjectsView.vue`

### [P0] Hero Proof Artifact Undermines Core Positioning
- **What**: `HeroDashboard.vue` auto-cycles every 4 seconds with empty strings for title, narrative, y-axis, and legend. There are no dataset selector buttons or pause controls.
- **Why it matters**: Akbar's headline is "Business Analyst & Data Storyteller". Presenting an unlabelled, unexplained, spinning chart directly signals that the author cannot clearly communicate data.
- **Fix**: Add a clear dataset selector (MnR BI Ops, IndoBERT NLP, Wayang CV), explicit titles, metric units, axis scales, and an explanatory caption for each dataset.
- **Suggested command**: `$impeccable clarify components/HeroDashboard.vue`

### [P0] Rigid Scroll Snap Breaks Natural Browsing
- **What**: `global.css:89` enforces `scroll-snap-type: y mandatory;` on `html`, while sections in `HomeView.vue` use `snap-start` and `snap-center`.
- **Why it matters**: Mandatory scroll snapping traps user scrolling on trackpads and mousewheels, cuts off content when sections exceed viewport height (common on laptops and mobile), and creates a jarring, jerky browsing feel.
- **Fix**: Remove `scroll-snap-type: y mandatory;` from `html` and remove section snap constraints in `HomeView.vue`. Let the page scroll naturally.
- **Suggested command**: `$impeccable layout assets/styles/global.css`

### [P1] Architectural Conflict: Full Scroller vs Tabbed Application
- **What**: `HomeView.vue` contains a full scroll experience with Hero, Interactive Career Map, and summary sections for Experience, Projects, Education, and Skills. Simultaneously, the Navbar exposes individual tabs for each section that load near-identical content.
- **Why it matters**: Visitors get disoriented. Clicking "Experience" in the navbar switches tabs and shows 3 cards, but scrolling down on "Home" already showed 2 of those exact same cards. "Explore full history →" links on Home switch tabs without clear back affordances.
- **Fix**: Either make Home a concise executive summary that clearly links into deep-dive tabs, or commit to a unified single-page scroll with smooth hash navigation.
- **Suggested command**: `$impeccable distill`

### [P1] Incomplete Execution of "The Trader's Ledger" Visual World
- **What**: DESIGN.md explicitly banished frosted glass as an anti-reference, promising warm dark ledger paper, rubber stamps, hairline rules, and ink figures. The current codebase still relies heavily on `bg-glass`, `backdrop-filter: blur(18px)`, and generic pill tags.
- **Why it matters**: The site looks like another generic dark-mode crypto/SaaS template rather than an intentional, distinctive, bespoke portfolio.
- **Fix**: Replace `bg-glass` with solid warm elevated surfaces (`bg-ink-2`, `bg-charcoal`), introduce the authored `.stamp` badge styling, and apply hairline ruled ledger separators.
- **Suggested command**: `$impeccable bolder`

## Persona Red Flags

### Recruiter-on-a-clock (3 min scan)
- **Hero Failure**: Looks at the chart on the right; it changes every 4 seconds without any text explaining what is being measured. Cannot tell what 94% or 112% represents. Moves on.
- **Projects Failure**: Glances at the bottom of the project cards to find metrics; sees a blank space where "RCA" and "91%" should be because of the black-on-black contrast bug.
- **Navigation Confusion**: Clicks "Experience" in navbar, then clicks ALB logo, lands back on Home, scrolls down and sees Experience again. Wonders why the site has duplicate pages.

### Senior Data Analyst / Peer (judging technical depth)
- **Chart Rigor**: Inspects `HeroDashboard`. No axis labels, no units, no sample size, no legend. Concludes the dashboard is aesthetic decoration rather than real analytical work.
- **Scroll Jank**: Attempts to scroll smoothly down the page on a high-DPI mouse or MacBook trackpad; `scroll-snap-type: y mandatory` jerks the viewport violently between sections.

### Mobile Visitor (on small screen)
- **Dock Contrast**: In `Navbar.vue:58`, touching an icon can trigger `hover:text-ink`, making the icon disappear into the dark dock background.
- **Viewport Overflow**: Long sections with `snap-always min-h-dvh` cause content to be clipped below the fold without the ability to scroll freely to read paragraphs.
